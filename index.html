How I work

Failure is data when the trace is complete.

The common loop across ARC, reinforcement learning, accelerator training, and CUDA is to make every decision observable, then promote only changes supported by replay.

InstrumentRecord requests, reasoning, actions, transitions, profiles, seeds, source revisions, and exact artifacts.
LocalizeFind the earliest wrong belief or bottleneck instead of optimizing the final symptom.
ExternalizeMove reusable structure into parsers, verifiers, world maps, kernels, or executable programs.
ReplayCheck the proposed fix against every preserved transition or workload before spending another full run.
PromoteRequire fresh-seed, fresh-game, or full-budget evidence and keep failed ideas in the research record.
